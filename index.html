Thunder Bay Civic Map

Source & data

A scalable viewer over the City's open data and other public sources: switch on layers from any category below, or combine several at once. Every dataset is a plain, directly-fetchable file — see the repo if you want to reuse the data yourself.

Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors. Police incidents: Thunder Bay Police Service via CityProtect (non-commercial research use), approximate/aggregated locations. Fire stations, fire zones, hydrants, trees, and the heritage register: City of Thunder Bay Open Data Portal. Restaurant listings: Justthemenu.ca (not affiliated). Highway cameras: Ontario 511 (Ministry of Transportation). Trails: OpenStreetMap contributors, via the Overpass API. Live flights: OpenSky Network. Live ships: AISStream.io. Full sources, scripts and data: github.com/Jandoue/Thunder-Bay.
